The Art of Application

"The efficacy of a product is heavily dependent on how it's applied. Taking the time to press, pat, and massage formulations into the skin not only enhances absorption but turns a routine into a grounding ritual."

1

Warm the Product

Always activate botanical oils and serums by gently warming them between your fingertips before applying.

2

Press, Don't Rub

Firmly press the product into your skin. This technique pushes active ingredients deeper and prevents micro-tears.

3

Upward Sweeps

Counteract gravity by always massaging the face and neck using gentle, upward sweeping motions.
